# 🏍️ Charlotte's Moto & Car Rush: City Dodge & Racers 🏎️💨

Welcome Charlotte! This is a complete, fully operational arcade web browser game custom built just for you!

You can play immediately locally in **any web browser** (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, Safari) on your computer, laptop, or tablet.

---

## 🚀 How to Play Right Now!

There are two easy ways to launch the game:

### Option 1 (Easiest - One Click!)
1. Double-click the file named **`run_game.bat`** in this folder.
2. It will automatically open the game in your default web browser!

### Option 2 (Direct Open)
1. Double-click **`index.html`** (or drag it into your browser window).
2. That's it! The game starts instantly with zero installation or setup required.

## 🏙️ Mode 1: City Dodge (Main Game)

- **The Man on the Motorcycle**: You start riding your agile motorcycle through the bustling city.
- **The 5 Traffic Cars**: Watch out! There are 5 cars cruising around the avenues and intersections:
  - 🔵 **Blue Cruiser**
  - 🔴 **Red Speeder**
  - 🟢 **Green Hatchback**
  - 🟡 **Yellow Taxi**
  - ⚫ **Black Muscle Car**
- **3 Lives / Hearts**: You start with 3 lives. If you get hit by one of the 5 cars, you lose 1 life!
  - You get **2 seconds of invulnerability (flashing)** after a hit so you have time to steer away!
  - If you run out of lives, don't worry: **all your points and money are 100% saved!**
- **Yellow Orbs & Points**: Drive into glowing yellow orbs scattered along roads, parks, and sidewalks to collect **Points**!
- **Minimap**: The radar in the bottom-left shows you (cyan), yellow orbs (gold), and the 5 cars (colored dots)!
